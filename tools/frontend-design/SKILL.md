# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a premier design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is never mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid cliché, templated, or generic AI defaults: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to the brief.

## Ground Designs in Subject Matter
- Identify the domain's vernacular, materials, and emotional tone. For healthcare, nutrition, and Nusantara sovereignty, use deep authoritative tones (Medical Teal `#0D9488`, Midnight Slate `#0A0F1D`, Amber Gold `#F59E0B`).
- Build with authentic domain data, real laboratory compositions, and actual clinical standards.

## Core Design Principles
1. **Hero with Intent:** Open with the most characteristic and striking element (live visual demo, interactive moment, or bold statement).
2. **Distinctive Typography:** Use deliberate typography (e.g. Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Outfit). Use tabular numbers (`font-mono`) for precision data. Limit line lengths to < 80 characters for optimal readability.
3. **Avoid AI Clichés:**
   - Avoid generic warm cream `#F4F1EA` or plain purple gradients everywhere.
   - Avoid highlighting just one single word in italics/color in headlines.
   - Avoid unnecessary all-caps labels or arbitrary numbered markers (01/02/03) unless actually sequential.
4. **Visual Structure is Information:** Outlines, borders, badges, and dividers must encode clinical/functional meaning rather than mere decoration.
5. **Purposeful Micro-Interactions:** Non-user-triggered animations should be used sparingly. Use motion that responds to user actions (gauges moving, cards expanding, tabs switching).
