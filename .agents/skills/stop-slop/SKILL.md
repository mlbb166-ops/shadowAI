# Stop Slop

Eliminate predictable AI writing patterns and robotic filler from documentation, user-facing copy, and technical explanations.

## Core Rules

1. **Cut filler phrases:** Remove throat-clearing openers ("It is important to remember that", "In today's fast-paced world", "Delve into"), empty emphasis ("crucial", "testament to", "game changer"), and excessive adverbs.
2. **Break formulaic structures:** Avoid forced binary contrasts ("Not only X, but also Y"), negative listings ("This is not just A, it is B"), rhetorical setups, and attributing human actions to inanimate objects (false agency).
3. **Use active human voice:** Every sentence needs a clear subject doing an action. Avoid passive constructions.
4. **Be specific and factual:** State exact data, standards, and metrics (e.g. "WHO 2006 Box-Cox LMS", "TKPI Kemenkes RI 2020", "2.2g DHA") instead of vague abstractions ("comprehensive solutions").
5. **Vary rhythm and length:** Mix short and medium sentences naturally. State facts directly without defensive qualifiers or excessive adjectives.
